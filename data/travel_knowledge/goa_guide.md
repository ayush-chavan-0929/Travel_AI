# Goa Travel Knowledge Base

## Regional Division
Goa is divided into two distinct districts: North Goa and South Goa.
- **North Goa**: Known for vibrant nightlife, bustling beach shacks, water sports, and party destinations.
  - Key beaches: Baga, Calangute, Anjuna, Vagator, Candolim, Morjim.
  - Attractions: Chapora Fort (Dil Chahta Hai fort), Aguada Fort, Saturday Night Market (Arpora), Tito's Lane.
  - Best for: Groups of friends, water sports enthusiasts, youth, nightlife lovers.
- **South Goa**: Known for serene, clean, uncrowded beaches, luxury resorts, and Portuguese heritage.
  - Key beaches: Palolem, Colva, Benaulim, Agonda, Butterfly Beach.
  - Attractions: Basilica of Bom Jesus, Se Cathedral, Dudhsagar Waterfalls.
  - Best for: Families, couples, honeymooners, peace seekers.

## Water Sports & Activities
- Top water sports at Baga & Calangute: Parasailing, Jet Skiing, Banana Boat Rides, Bumper Rides.
- Average cost for water sports 5-ride combo: ₹1,200 to ₹1,800 per person.
- Scuba diving & island trips: Grand Island trip with dolphin safari (~₹2,000/person).
- Sunset cruises: Mandovi River Cruise (~₹500/person).

## Budget Guidelines
- Budget stay (hostels / 2-star): ₹1,000 - ₹2,000 per room/night.
- Mid-range (3-star with pool): ₹2,500 - ₹4,500 per room/night.
- Luxury (4-star & 5-star beachfront): ₹6,000 - ₹18,000 per room/night.
- Food & dining: Beach shacks average ₹500 - ₹800/person per meal.
- Local travel: Two-wheeler scooter rental: ₹400 - ₹600/day. Self-drive car: ₹1,500 - ₹2,500/day.

## Travel Timing & Seasonality
- Peak season: November to February (great weather, sunny, vibrant, expensive).
- Shoulder season: October and March (pleasant, economical, fewer crowds).
- Monsoon season: June to September (lush green, serene, sea swimming closed).
